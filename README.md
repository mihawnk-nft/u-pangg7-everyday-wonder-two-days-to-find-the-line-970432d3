Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/c/u-pangg7-everyday-wonder-two-days-to-find-the-line-970432d3)
competition **Everyday Wonder, Two Days to Find the Line**: one sentence,
at most 200 characters, on a small moment of wonder in everyday life,
judged pairwise by an AI judge that rewards surprise.

## The sentence

> The lift mirror caught me mid-yawn, and for a second I apologised to a stranger who turned out to be me.

104 characters. A lift ride, a yawn, a mirror — an ordinary moment turned
at an angle the reader has not met before: apologising to a stranger who
turns out to be yourself. The twist lands in the final clause and
re-frames everything before it, in a single clear read.

## How it works

`main.py` (pure Python standard library, no dependencies, no network)
writes the sentence to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (or
`/output/entry.txt` in the grading sandbox).

```sh
python3 main.py
```
